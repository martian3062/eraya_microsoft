x402 payments

Quant Desk — autonomous trading
The swarm trades CSPR on live exchange prices with its own quant ensemble (SMA crossover · RSI · Bollinger · momentum · vol targeting). You set one risk dial; Guardian enforces the derived envelope; every fill settles as a real Casper testnet transfer. Voice-controlled: “set risk to seven”, “start autopilot”.
Odra smart contracts
AgentRegistry (on-chain agent identity + reputation) and TradePolicy (risk envelope + trade record computed on-chain) — authored with MockVM test suites, deploying to testnet.
